# Stochastic Processes and Renewal Theory

### IIT Bombay – Summer Research Fellowship 2024

**Author:** Ananya Hazarika  
**Report Date:** August 8, 2024  
**Research Area:** Probability Theory, Stochastic Processes, Renewal Theory and Insurance Mathematics

## Overview

This project presents a mathematical study of fundamental concepts in stochastic processes and renewal theory, with applications to insurance risk modelling.

The study explores the theoretical foundations of probability, measure theory, Poisson processes and renewal processes. Particular attention is given to the Cramér–Lundberg risk model, discounted claim processes, renewal equations and delayed renewal models.

The objective was to develop a deeper understanding of stochastic modelling techniques and their applications in analysing random events, claim arrivals and financial risk.

## Mathematical Significance

The study examines how stochastic processes can model random events occurring over time.

Renewal theory provides a framework for analysing recurring events, while Poisson processes offer a mathematical foundation for modelling random arrivals.

In insurance mathematics, these concepts are useful for understanding claim frequency, interarrival times, expected liabilities and the long-term behaviour of risk processes.

The study also explores how Laplace–Stieltjes transforms simplify the analysis of probability distributions and renewal equations.
